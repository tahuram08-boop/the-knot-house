WHO WE ARE

A Small Idea With A Big Handmade Heart

The Knot House was created around a simple idea — to bring beautiful crochet creations into one welcoming online space.

Crochet is not simply about yarn and stitches. It is about patience, creativity, imagination and the personal touch behind every handmade piece.

Our website is designed to make it easier for crochet lovers to discover different creations, explore their details and find something that feels truly special.

🧶
Every loop tells a story. From the first loop to the final stitch, handmade crochet carries the time, creativity and care behind its creation.
